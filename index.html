01 — FEATURED DISASTER AI ARCHITECTURE SIH 2026 [PS 26001]

NER Landslide Early Warning System

AI-based early warning & landslide risk monitoring system across the North Eastern Region of India (Smart India Hackathon 2026)

PROBLEM STATEMENT

The North Eastern Region (NER) of India is critically vulnerable to rainfall-triggered landslides and slope instability due to fragile terrain and intense monsoons. Existing disaster monitoring was reactive, fragmented, and heavily reliant on slow manual reporting, causing transit blockages, isolated communities, and delayed emergency response.

ARCHITECTURAL SOLUTION

Engineered an AI-driven early-warning pipeline integrating NASA Global Landslide Catalogs, Geological Survey of India (GSI) geospatial inventories, and live OpenWeather precipitation forecasts. Powered by XGBoost and Scikit-learn for slope risk classification and YOLO computer vision for debris/crack detection. Features interactive Leaflet/Folium GIS risk heatmaps, Groq GenAI risk explanations, and automated Twilio SMS & SMTP emergency dispatch.

GITHUB REPO DISCUSS ARCHITECTURE

ENGINEERING CHALLENGES

  • Integrating disparate multi-source data streams (satellite, GSI catalogs, rainfall forecasts) into normalized risk feature vectors.
  • Developing low-latency edge inference for computer vision (YOLO) detecting visual indicators such as road blockages and surface fissures.
  • Implementing a resilient alert queue to ensure automated Twilio SMS notifications dispatch during regional network cut-offs.

DATA PIPELINE FLOW

NASA & GSI Data → OpenWeather API → Feature Engineering → XGBoost & YOLO → Leaflet GIS Map → Twilio Alert

ARCHITECTURAL SOLUTION

Conducted comprehensive data cleaning, outlier treatment, and statistical correlation analysis across key pollutants (PM2.5, PM10, NO2, CO). Hospital Management SQL Architecture

Relational database schema, multi-table joins, subqueries, and operational healthcare telemetry in MySQL

PROBLEM STATEMENT

Healthcare facilities struggled with disconnected records between patient admissions, doctor allocations, diagnosis histories, and billing telemetry, resulting in data redundancy, slow query latency, and appointment scheduling friction.

ARCHITECTURAL SOLUTION

Engineered a normalized 3NF relational database schema in MySQL linking 5+ transactional clinical tables. Formulated advanced multi-table INNER/LEFT joins, correlated subqueries, window functions, and indexing strategies to deliver instantaneous administrative reporting and patient lifecycle tracking.

  • Formulating aggregate analytical queries to track doctor load factors and bed utilization rates for hospital administration.
